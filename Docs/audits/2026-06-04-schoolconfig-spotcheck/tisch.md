### Notes / caveats for downstream reviewers
- **Dance department** is a school-wide exception to several caps the config treats uniformly: it grants **no** AP/IB advanced-standing credit and caps college-in-HS credit at 8 (L564); transfer credit in the major is at chair discretion (L355). The single school-level `creditCaps.advanced_standing=32` does not capture this. Per-program override may be needed.
- `degreeType` and the two `? unverifiable` residency fields (`minCredits=64`, `majorMinorResidencyPercent=50`) are the rows most worth a human decision: they are inherited CAS values presented as bulletin-claimed but lack Tisch bulletin support.
