## Notes / scope caveats not captured by the config

- **DAUS (Division of Applied Undergraduate Studies) divergences are unmodeled.** SPS has effectively two regimes. The config encodes only the **Schack/Tisch** numbers. DAUS bachelor's: residency **48** cr (not 64, L646); advanced-standing cap **80** (not 64, L378); associate's: residency **50% of total** (L638) and advanced-standing **30** (L378). If the engine applies `sps.json` to DAUS programs (Humanities BA, Social Sciences BA, Liberal Arts AA, the AAS programs), residency/transfer caps will be wrong for those students.
- **"A second major outside of SPS can only be pursued within the College of Arts and Science."** (L607) — a cross-school constraint not represented in `doubleCounting`.
- **Auto-dismissal triggers** (fail a required course twice → dismissal, L300/L479) are not modeled; out of scope for these fields.
