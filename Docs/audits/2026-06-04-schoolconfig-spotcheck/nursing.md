## Caveats / items the config does NOT model (potential gaps, not errors in existing fields)

- **Per-course C floor in science & nursing sequence drives progression/dismissal**, somewhat independent of the 2.0 GPA: "Receiving a grade below C, or an IF, in any science or nursing sequence course" → academic notice; "If a passing grade of C is not achieved upon re-taking any science or nursing sequence course" → dismissal; "Failure of two science or nursing sequence courses" → dismissal. Captured partly by `gradeThresholds`, but the progression/dismissal automaton is not represented.
- **Six-year completion limit**: "All course requirements must be completed within six years from the date of matriculation." No `timeLimitYears`-type field in config (consistent with the repo's earlier removal of that field, per PR-A).
- **Latin honors "58 points in residence"**: distinct from the 32-credit degree residency; config models neither Latin-honors thresholds (correctly, since they're distribution-based) nor the 58-point honors residency.
- **Writing Proficiency Exam** for transfer students (EXPOS-UA placement) — admissions/placement logic, out of config scope.
